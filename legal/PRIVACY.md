This policy explains what Radiator collects, how we use it, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Content you post:** reviews, maintenance issues, questions, building names, and
  photos. Please don't include personal contact details — we automatically strip
  obvious phone numbers and emails from submitted text, but you should not rely on it.
- **Account/profile info:** a display handle you choose, and (if you buy access) payment
  details handled by our payment processor — we do not store full card numbers.
- **Basic technical data:** IP address and request information, used for security, rate-
  limiting, and preventing abuse. [Update if you add analytics or cookies.]

## What we do NOT try to collect
Radiator is designed to show tenant experience in aggregate. We ask you not to submit
other people's personal information, unit numbers, or the names of individuals who
aren't the landlord/management company.

## How we use it
- To operate Radiator: display shared reviews and records to all visitors, run the
  give-to-get and paid-access features, and keep the service secure.
- To moderate content and enforce our Community Guidelines.
- To respond to reports, corrections, and support requests.

## How content is shared
Reviews, issues, questions, names, and photos you post are **public** — they are shown
to everyone who uses Radiator. Post accordingly. Your display handle appears with your
content; your email (if any) is not shown publicly.

## Photos
We recommend we strip location metadata (EXIF) from uploaded photos before display.
[Confirm this is implemented in your image pipeline before relying on it.] Don't upload
photos that reveal a specific unit number or another person's private information.

## Retention
We keep content while it's live on Radiator. You can request removal of content you
posted, and we'll honor reasonable requests unless we must keep it for legal reasons.
