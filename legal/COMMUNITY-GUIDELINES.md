# Radiator Community Guidelines

Radiator exists to put honest, useful information in renters' hands. These rules
keep it trustworthy and fair to everyone — tenants and landlords alike.

## Write from real experience
Only review a building or unit you have actually lived in, applied to, toured, or
otherwise have firsthand knowledge of. Radiator is for real tenant experience, not
rumor or secondhand stories.

## Be specific, and separate fact from opinion
The most helpful reviews describe what happened: "The heat was out for six days in
January and it took three calls to get it fixed." That's more useful — and safer —
than a bare insult. Share your honest opinion, but ground it in your experience.

## What you may not post
- **Personal information.** No phone numbers, emails, home addresses of individuals,
  apartment/unit numbers, or other private details of any person (yours or anyone
  else's). We automatically strip obvious contact info, but don't rely on that.
- **Names of individuals** who aren't the landlord/management company — e.g. don't
  name a maintenance worker, a leasing agent, or another tenant.
- **Harassment, threats, or doxxing** of anyone.
- **Discriminatory content** — no attacks on, or characterizations of, residents or
  neighborhoods based on race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, disability,
  family status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or source of income.
- **Retaliation or fake reviews** — don't post reviews you know to be false, and
  don't post to retaliate rather than inform. Landlords may not post fake positive
  reviews of their own buildings or fake negative reviews of competitors.
- **Illegal content, spam, or advertising.**

## Official records are reports, not verdicts
City of Chicago violation and complaint data shown on Radiator reflects what has been
*reported to or recorded by* the city. A recorded complaint is not proof of
wrongdoing, and an open violation may already be in the process of being fixed.

## Landlords and management: right of reply
If you own or manage a building on Radiator, you can respond publicly to reviews and
request corrections of factual errors through the reporting tools. We take good-faith
correction requests seriously.

## Reporting and moderation
Anyone can flag a review, issue, question, or photo with the **Report** control.
Flagged content is reviewed against these guidelines and may be removed. We may remove
content, and limit or remove accounts, that violate these rules.

## Corrections and removals
If something about you or your building is inaccurate, use the report tool or contact
us. We'll review it promptly and correct or remove content that violates these
guidelines or is shown to be false.
